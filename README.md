# Bloodlot Ascension

A dark fantasy roguelike where every blade is a roll and every roll is a prayer.
Weapons and accessories drop at rarities from Common to Ascended (1 in 100,000) and Transcendent (1 in 1,000,000).

## Play or download

| Platform | How |
|---|---|
| **Browser (any device)** | Play online: https://samarksurvival-bit.github.io/bloodlot-ascension/ |
| **PC (Windows, Mac, Linux)** | Download **Bloodlot-Ascension-PC-vX.zip** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest), unzip, open `Bloodlot-Ascension.html` |
| **Android** | Download **Bloodlot-Ascension-Android-vX.apk** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and install it (allow installing apps from your browser when asked) |
| **iPhone / iPad** | Open the online link in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen** |

Progress is saved on your device. Patch notes are in the game: **What's new** on the title screen.

## Controls

| PC | Phone | Action |
|---|---|---|
| WASD | drag anywhere | move |
| Mouse / Space | Attack button | attack (auto-aims on phone) |
| Shift | Dash | dash (needs a dash skill) |
| Q | God | god ability |
| R | Roll | roll a Fate Die |
| F | Use | shrines, altars, chests, stairs |
| 1–6 · I · P | potions · Bag · ☰ | potions · inventory · pause |
